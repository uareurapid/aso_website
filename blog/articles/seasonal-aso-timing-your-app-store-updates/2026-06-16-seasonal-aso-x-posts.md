## Post 2 — The Calendar (Value Thread)

The App Store has 4 major seasonal windows. Most devs compete for the same keywords all year.

Thread: How to map your year in 60 seconds 🧵

1/ Winter Holidays (Nov–Dec)
   New devices, gift card redemptions. Peak download window of the year.
   → Rotate in gift/holiday keywords. Update screenshots with subtle seasonal cues.

2/ New Year Resolution (Jan–Feb)
   "Habit tracker" search volume spikes 3-5x. Biggest organic opportunity.
   → Ship your keyword rotation by Dec 28. Not Jan 2.

3/ Back to School (Aug–Sep)
   Second-largest seasonal spike. Students flood the store.
   → Even productivity apps have hidden student relevance. Check your keyword set.

4/ Summer Slump (Jun–Aug)
   Downloads dip. Most devs pause ASO.
   → Don't pause. Test. This is the best time to experiment — less competitive noise.

## Post 3 — The Specific Tip (High-Value Action)

The single highest-leverage seasonal ASO move:

Change your App Store subtitle 1–2 weeks before a seasonal spike.

One line of text. Zero dev work. Measurable ranking impact.

Example for a habit tracker in January:
❌ "Daily Habit Tracker & Routine Builder"
✅ "New Year Goal Tracker & Daily Habits"

Same app. Different timing. Different results.
